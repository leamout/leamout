Voice AI systems have to solve two different problems: generating an intelligent response and maintaining a reliable telephone call. Treating the call as an afterthought makes control, interoperability, and observability harder.

## The signaling and media boundary

SIP negotiates and manages sessions; RTP carries audio. Real-time AI introduces another processing path for speech detection, transcription, reasoning, and synthesis. An agent runtime needs a clear boundary between these concerns.

Leamout's architecture uses OpenSIPS for SIP signaling, FreeSWITCH for call and media control, RTPengine for RTP handling, and Go services to orchestrate sessions and agents. These are architectural choices, not a claim of production availability.

## Why bring your own carrier?

Organizations may already have SIP trunks, numbers, or PBX infrastructure. BYOC keeps that connectivity under customer control while the agent runtime focuses on streaming media and AI orchestration.

## What we're still building

Leamout is under active development, and the backend has not yet been publicly deployed. The difficult work is not simply connecting an LLM to audio; it is managing interruptions, tool execution, state recovery, and call lifecycle events predictably.

We'll document those trade-offs as the system develops.
